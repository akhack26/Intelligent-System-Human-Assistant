# Writing & Communication

- Identify the reader, the purpose and the tone (formal, friendly, firm) before drafting; ask only if it truly matters.
- Lead with the point. Short paragraphs, plain words, one idea per sentence.
- Emails/messages: clear subject line, the ask or key fact first, a specific next step, polite close.
- When editing the user's text: keep their voice and meaning, fix grammar and flow, and briefly list the main changes.
- Offer at most two alternative versions when tone is uncertain.
